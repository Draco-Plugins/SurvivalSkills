I want to create an item teleporting system for storage transfer improvements

The player will craft a high tech wrench that allows them to crouch and left click a chest and attach a item transfer pipe (player must have crafted item transfer pipes as well and have them in their inventory). Since they just placed the pipe, that pipe will be "selected". If the player left clicks that chest again while crouching they will remove the transfer pipe. Left clicking the chest again (no crouch) will select a chest again if either a 60 second timer runs out from initial selection or the player walks 250 blocks away from the chest (this 250 range also includes the maximum distance a sending and receiving pipe can be from each other). The player should be messaged when the cooldown has 30 seconds left, 10 seconds left, 3 seconds left, and no time left. If the player is no longer holding the wrench then the timer just gets cancelled with no messages.

With a pipe attached to a chest and the pipe "selected", if a player left clicks a chest without any pipe on it then they will attach a pipe to that chest and it will be a "receiver" chest. This will immediately open up a 9 slot inventory that can be used as a white list filter, no items indicating that any item can enter this chest. If the player puts an item in it "copies" the item like the auto trash does. This also resets the 60 second cooldown. Similarly as the sender pipe, if the player shift and left clicks a receiver chest then it will remove the receiver.

When the player holds the wrench in their hand, any of their chests that have pipes in them will add block highlights to the chests with blue indicating a sender chest and green indicating a receiver chest. Their can be multiple receiver pipes per sender pipe but only one sender pipe per receiver pipe. All of these pipe locations and whitelists and pipe ownerships (using UUID) should be stored in a YAML file

Help me brainstorm the crafting recipes and features for this.
